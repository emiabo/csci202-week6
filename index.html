How to play

  • Keep shoppers out of your bubble. The closer they get, the faster it fills.
  • Grab everything on the shopping list (bottom of the screen), then head for the 🚪 checkout.
  • A full bubble costs a ❤️. Lose all three and you're contaminated.
  • Watch for 💬: close talkers are coming over for a chat.

Best score: 0

Controls

  • Drag your bubble to move it, or drag anywhere else to steer without covering it.
  • Arrow keys or WASD also work.
  • P or Esc pauses, M mutes.
  • F or a two-finger tap toggles reduced flash mode (on by default if your system asks for reduced motion).

What is this?

A simulation about personal space. Inspired by professional guidance on a certain microbe.

How does this work?

The project is written in p5.js, with Perlin noise used for movement physics, the cycling color ring, and the random bars.
